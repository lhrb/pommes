# How to Win

A game lasts seven days 20160 ticks. When the week ends, the Pommesbude with the most **Prestige** is crowned **Die goldene Pommes**.

Money keeps your business running, but money alone does not win the game. Prestige is earned by becoming notable: dominating parts of the market, developing your shop, building loyal customers and making bold moves against your competition.

## Serve the Market

Every demographic wants something different.

Adjust your dishes, prices and portions to fit the current market. A perfect menu for students may perform badly with retirees or foodies. Market conditions change throughout the week, so no menu remains optimal forever.

Strong performance can earn Prestige through:

* High market share within a demographic
* Best-selling dishes
* Large numbers of portions sold
* Exceptional earnings

Specializing may help you dominate one audience. A broader menu may provide more stability when the market changes.

## Build a Reputation

Regular customers are valuable beyond their immediate purchases.

Attracting regulars and keeping them satisfied can become an important source of Prestige. A shop with loyal customers may perform more consistently, while aggressive competitors depend more heavily on changing market conditions.

## Choose Your Investments

Research improves your Pommesbude permanently for the current game.

There are more research opportunities than you can fully develop during one week. Choose which parts of your business matter most to your strategy. Being ahead of your competitors in a research field may also increase your reputation.

The Black Market offers powerful alternatives. These upgrades may create unusual advantages, untaxed income or protection from certain problems—but they can also attract attention.

You will not be able to master everything. Decide what kind of Pommesbude you want to build.

## Take Action

Actions temporarily change the market or interfere with another player.

You may perform at most **two actions every twelve hours**. Choosing the correct moment is therefore more important than acting constantly.

Actions can be used to:

* Attract a demographic
* Change local demand
* Improve a temporary opportunity
* Disrupt a competitor
* Challenge the current leader

Bold actions against strong opponents can earn more Prestige than attacking weaker shops. However, an expensive action is only worthwhile when you can take advantage of its effect.

## Keep Your Books—or Do Not

Untaxed income can accelerate your growth, but suspicious businesses may be audited.

A successful shortcut may provide money for research, actions or expansion. An audit may remove those gains and damage your reputation.

The safest business does not always win. Neither does the greediest.

## Find Your Strategy

There is no single path to victory.

You might:

* Dominate one valuable demographic
* Adapt your menu whenever the market changes
* Develop the best research operation
* Build a large base of regular customers
* Use actions to challenge the strongest players
* Take risks through the Black Market
* Operate an enormous, efficient business
* Combine several smaller advantages

Watch the market, watch your competitors and spend your money with purpose.

At the end of the week, Prestige decides who serves the best Pommes in the city.
